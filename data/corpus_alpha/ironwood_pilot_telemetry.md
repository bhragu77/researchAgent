# Ironwood Pilot Telemetry Summary

The Ironwood pilot ran at the Doncaster distribution centre for fourteen weeks.
Robotic picking throughput averaged 412 lines per hour against a manual baseline
of 173 lines per hour on comparable order profiles.

Pick accuracy during the pilot reached 99.2 percent, compared with the 96.6
percent baseline implied by the 3.4 percent manual error rate. Exceptions were
dominated by mis-scanned barcodes on shrink-wrapped multipacks.

Robot utilisation averaged 61 percent, below the vendor's modelled 80 percent.
The shortfall traces to aisle congestion during replenishment windows rather than
to robot availability.
