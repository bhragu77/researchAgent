# Robotics Safety and Commissioning Standard

All goods-to-person robotics deployed at Northwind Manufacturing must pass a
commissioning safety review before live operation. The review covers emergency
stop coverage, pedestrian segregation, and load stability at height.

A qualified safety engineer must sign off each aisle individually. Northwind
currently employs two such engineers, which caps commissioning throughput at
roughly forty aisles per quarter.

Robotic picking cells operating above four metres require fall-arrest netting
under the Northwind standard, adding approximately GBP 38,000 per aisle that is
routinely excluded from vendor quotations.

Insurance premiums increase by 4 percent for the first year of any automated
materials handling deployment, reverting to baseline after an incident-free year.
