# Project Ironwood: Warehouse Picking Automation

Northwind Manufacturing operates seven regional distribution centres handling a
catalogue of 18,500 active SKUs. Order picking is manual and paper-driven, and
the measured picking error rate is 3.4 percent of line items.

Project Ironwood proposes goods-to-person robotic picking at the two highest
volume centres. Modelled annual savings are GBP 620,000 against an
implementation cost of GBP 1,450,000. Payback is estimated at 27 months.

The binding constraint is dock door capacity, not capital. Ironwood assumes no
more than one centre is converted per financial year, because each conversion
removes a centre from the network for eleven weeks.

Northwind's chilled goods division is explicitly out of scope for Ironwood:
sub-zero operating temperatures void the robotics vendor warranty.
