# Project Lantern: KYC Onboarding Automation

Sterling Retail Bank processes 96,000 personal account openings per year across
its branch and digital channels. Average end-to-end onboarding time is 4.2 days,
against a challenger-bank benchmark of 11 minutes for digital-only journeys.

Project Lantern proposes automated document verification and sanctions screening
at the point of application. Modelled annual savings are GBP 385,000 against an
implementation cost of GBP 240,000. Payback is estimated at 9 months.

The constraint is regulatory: the Financial Conduct Authority requires a manual
review step for any applicant flagged as a politically exposed person, and
Lantern does not seek to remove that step.

Sterling's commercial banking arm is out of scope for Lantern. Its onboarding is
governed by a separate correspondent banking policy.
