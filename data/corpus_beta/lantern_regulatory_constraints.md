# Onboarding Regulatory Constraints

Sterling Retail Bank operates under FCA supervision, and all customer onboarding
automation must preserve auditability of every identity decision for six years.

Automated sanctions screening must escalate any partial name match above a 0.85
similarity threshold to a human analyst. Sterling's compliance function currently
staffs eleven analysts across two shifts.

Any change to the onboarding journey requires a Consumer Duty impact assessment
before release. These assessments take four to six weeks and are the dominant
schedule risk for Project Lantern.

Sterling may not store identity document images outside the United Kingdom under
its current data processing agreement.
