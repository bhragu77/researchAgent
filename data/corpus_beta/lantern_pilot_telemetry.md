# Lantern Pilot Telemetry Summary

The Lantern pilot processed 4,300 digital applications over nine weeks. Automated
document verification cleared 78 percent of applications without analyst touch.

Median onboarding time for automated journeys fell to 19 minutes from the 4.2 day
branch baseline. Applications routed to manual review averaged 2.1 days.

False positive rates on sanctions screening ran at 12 percent, above the 6 percent
target. Tuning the similarity threshold is deferred until the Consumer Duty impact
assessment completes.
